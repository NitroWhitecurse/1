# Shop Policies Reference

Used by `customer-support` for consistent, on-brand answers. Edit to match your actual policies.

## Turnaround
- Standard orders: 5-7 business days from payment to shipment.
- Rush orders: 2-3 business days for a surcharge (see business-data/pricing.md).

## Revisions
- One free minor revision (color/scale/orientation) before printing starts.
- Design changes after printing has started are billed at the design hourly rate.

## Refunds & reprints
- Print arrives damaged or defective (not due to customer misuse): free reprint or full refund, customer's choice.
- Customer wants a different design after receiving a correct print: no refund, but 20% off a reprint.
- Shipping damage: request photos, file a carrier claim, reprint and reship at no cost while claim is pending.

## Escalation rules for customer-support agent
Do NOT auto-send a reply and stop there — draft it for review, and flag to the owner immediately (don't just draft) when:
- The customer is asking for a refund over $50.
- The customer is angry, threatening a chargeback, or leaving a bad review.
- The issue involves a shipping claim or lost package.
- The request falls outside these policies and you're not sure how to answer.
